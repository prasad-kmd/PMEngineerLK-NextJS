Welcome to the Faculty of Engineering Technology at The Open University of Sri Lanka (OUSL). This guide provides essential information for students navigating the 2025/2026 academic year. OUSL operates on an Open and Distance Learning (ODL) philosophy, offering flexibility and lifelong learning opportunities.

2. Academic Structure

At OUSL, learning is measured in Credits and Levels. Each course has a unique code that identifies its department, category, level, and credit value.

Departmental Codes

  • AG: Agricultural and Plantation Engineering
  • CV: Civil Engineering
  • EE: Electrical and Computer Engineering
  • MH: Mathematics and Philosophy of Engineering
  • DM: Mechanical Engineering
  • TA: Textile and Apparel Technology

Course Categories

  • Z: Mathematics
  • B: Basic Science
  • K: Computing
  • S: Engineering Sciences/Design
  • Y: Engineering Projects
  • M: Management
  • J: General
  • I: Industrial

3. Credits and Workload

  • Theory-based courses: 1 Credit = 50 hours of learning.
  • Project/Training courses: 1 Credit = 100 hours of work.
  • Annual Limit: Maximum of 38 credits per academic year.
  • Minimum Enrolment: 8 credits for first-time students.

4. Assessment and GPA

Courses are evaluated through Continuous Assessment (CA) and a Final Evaluation (FE). A minimum Overall Continuous Assessment Mark (OCAM) is required to be eligible for the final exam.

5. Fee Structure (2025/2026)

Tuition fees are charged per credit based on the SLQF level of the course:

  • Level 2: Rs. 1,400 per credit
  • Level 3 & 4: Rs. 2,080 per credit
  • Level 5, 6 & 7: Rs. 3,220 per credit

Additional annual fees include Registration (Rs. 500), Facilities (Rs. 2,500), and a one-time Instrument Usage fee (Rs. 12,500).

6. Industrial Training

BSc Honours in Engineering and Bachelor of Industrial Studies students must complete 6 credits of industrial training (minimum 26 weeks). Training is managed by the Training Engineer and regulated by NAITA.

7. Learner Support

OUSL provides multiple layers of support:

  • LSC (Learner Support Cell): General guidance on ODL.
  • OULMS: Learning Management System for course materials.
  • Student Academic Counsellors: Department-specific assistance.
